01 / Brand idea

Fix the system. Free the subject.

Celesnity combines the institutional trust of currency with one living subject in motion. Structure repeats so the idea can move without losing its source.

01

Identity

The orbit and wordmark establish authorship before texture or imagery enters the frame.

02

Utility

Labels, serials and bands make the artifact feel accountable, legible and operational.

03

Anchor

One oversized numeral or rotated identifier gives every composition a fixed coordinate.

04

Life

A bird, insect, machine or human subject breaks the system with decisive movement.

02 / Logo system

The orbit leads. The wordmark confirms.

The inherited orbit remains the primary mark. Pair it with the CELESNITY wordmark in Inter Bold, keeping both elements clear of imagery and fine-line texture.

Approved use

Keep it stable

  • Keep clear space equal to half the orbit diameter.
  • Use the complete lockup at 120px wide or larger.
  • Use the orbit alone only at 24px or larger.

Misuse

Never alter the mark

  • Do not stretch, outline, rotate or recolor the orbit.
  • Do not place it directly over a busy subject.
  • Do not rebuild the wordmark with another typeface.

07 / Voice and messaging

Precise enough to trust. Human enough to move.

The voice pairs operational clarity with a single memorable idea. It names evidence directly and avoids inflated claims, unexplained jargon and decorative futurism.

01 / Direct

Lead with the outcome

Say what changed, what matters and what the reader can do next.

02 / Grounded

Separate proof from possibility

Describe known sources precisely. Label concepts, projections and prototypes honestly.

03 / Alive

Make one idea memorable

Pair concise institutional language with one vivid verb, subject or act of motion.

Approved headline

Proof in motion.

Short, active and ownable. The line connects trust with movement without overstating a claim.

Approved message pattern

See the source. Understand the system. Move with confidence.

Use a clear progression: evidence first, explanation second, action last. Prefer “Explore the system” or “View the source” to generic calls such as “Learn more.”
