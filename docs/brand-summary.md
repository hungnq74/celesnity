# Celesnity Brand Summary

## Position

**Industrial intelligence for the physical world.**

Celesnity connects people, machines and operational evidence through Minder. The brand should feel exact, consequential and human—never decorative technology for its own sake.

## Product architecture

- **Minder Platform** — shared operating context.
- **Minder Hardware** — voice, vision and machine signals.
- **Minder Research** — action–outcome evidence toward an industrial world model.

## Identity

The orbit mark establishes authorship; the Celesnity wordmark confirms it. Preserve clear space around the mark and avoid effects, distortion or decorative recolouring.

Maintained logo files:

- `celesnity-web/public/brand/orbit-ink.png`
- `celesnity-web/public/brand/orbit-white.png`

## Typography

- **Instrument Serif** — major statements and editorial contrast.
- **Inter Variable** — body copy and interface text.
- **Roboto Mono** — operational labels, indices and technical metadata.

The local Inter files live in `celesnity-web/app/fonts/`. Major statements should remain spacious and concise; body copy should explain rather than advertise.

## Core palette

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Midnight navy | `#030813` |
| Raised navy | `#0A1A2D` |
| Cyanotype teal | `#0798A8` |
| Signal cyan | `#32D8E6` |
| Ivory paper | `#F1EEE5` |
| Ink | `#0D1420` |
| Cobalt | `#2752FF` |
| Signal lime | `#C4FF3D` |

Use navy or ivory as the dominant field. Cobalt structures information; cyan and lime should mark live signals, approval or progress rather than fill large areas without meaning.

## Artwork

The production site maintains two approved directions:

1. **Celestial field** — scattered signals becoming shared context.
2. **Industrial blueprint** — physical infrastructure rendered as a precise operating system.

Artwork must preserve a calm text-safe region. Headlines remain crisp HTML text and must not be embedded in imagery. Grain supports materiality but must never reduce legibility.

## Voice

- Short, concrete and confident.
- Name the physical work, evidence and decision.
- Prefer one clear claim over a stack of slogans.
- Avoid unverified metrics, certification claims and inflated autonomy language.
- Keep people visibly responsible for consequential action.

Examples of the intended register:

- “Industrial intelligence for the physical world.”
- “How Minder is built.”
- “Start with one operation.”

## Maintained source of truth

Public landing-page copy is centralized in `celesnity-web/content/site.ts`. Visual tokens and responsive rules are maintained in `celesnity-web/app/globals.css`.
